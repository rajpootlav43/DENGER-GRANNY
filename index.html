CHUDAIL KA MAHAL

4 level hain! Mahal mein chabiyan aur ek bandook dhundho. Phir ya to chudail ko goli maar ke khatam karo (har level mein uska HP badhta hai) ya darwaza kholo aur bhaag niklo. Wo tumhe pakad le to tumhari jaan le legi! Chudail ko sab sunai deta hai.
Goli lagne par wo ruk jaati hai, HP khatam to mar jaati hai.
Upar-right gol map: neela chamakta ghera + safed teer = tum, 🟡 chabi, 🟦 bandook, 🟥 EXIT.
🎧 Headphones lagao! M = awaaz on/off.
PC: WASD chalo, mouse dekho, Shift daudo, click goli.
Mobile: buttons + screen par ungli ghumao.
